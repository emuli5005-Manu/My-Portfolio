EMMANUEL MULI — WEB & ICT

I’m an ICT student at The Kiambu National Polytechnic, pursuing a Diploma in ICT Technician, with a growing focus on building useful experiences for the web.

I create clean, modern websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I care about straightforward navigation, thoughtful details, and making sure a site works as well on a phone as it does on a desktop.

Alongside web development, I work with computer repair and network installation. I’ve also built a school management system for entering student data and helping students view their results.
